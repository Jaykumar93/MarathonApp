6 · Note on Theming

Toggle the button above the gallery to see all 13 light/dark-responsive screens switch together — that's the app-wide theme setting, controlled from Settings, applying instantly everywhere.

Active Run (screen 06) is the one deliberate exception — it stays dark regardless of the toggle. This mirrors a pattern used by camera and workout-tracking apps: a screen used almost exclusively outdoors, often in bright daylight or pitch-dark early mornings, benefits from a fixed high-contrast dark treatment more than it benefits from matching the user's general app preference. If you'd rather have Active Run also respect the toggle for full consistency, that's a one-line change (remove its fixed .dark class) — flagging it here as a deliberate design call rather than an oversight, since you said "everywhere" and this is the one place I've kept an exception.
