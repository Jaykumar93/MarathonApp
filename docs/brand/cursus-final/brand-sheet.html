Rules

Use Pika for

Onboarding, plan ready, empty states, the reflection step, personal bests, notifications and the splash screen.

Never use Pika for

Active run, errors, pain or niggle flags, anything about a missed run, or the paywall. Pika is never the one delivering bad news.
